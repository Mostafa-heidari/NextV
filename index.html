01 / ABOUT ME

Driven by purpose.
Powered by curiosity.

I’m an Iranian entrepreneur and co-founder of Ahangar, a technology-driven steel trading company.

My entrepreneurial journey began in 2019, when I co-founded the business with my brother and initially led its marketing activities. After gaining experience at Digikala, one of Iran’s leading e-commerce companies, I returned to Ahangar as CEO to help lead its next stage of growth.

During my tenure, Ahangar grew into a business generating over $10 million in annual revenue, with a portfolio of more than 10,000 products and strong year-over-year growth.

In 2026, I stepped away from day-to-day executive management to focus on business strategy and explore opportunities in international business, finance, and artificial intelligence.
